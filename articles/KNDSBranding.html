KNDS Branding

KNDS-LP 96.3 FM is North Dakota State University's student-run radio station. In the fall of 2026, the station had no student managers. My friends, Eli and Jonah, stepped up to become General and Assistant Managers. About a week later, I volunteered as KNDS's Treasurer/Secretary. This role later added on the responsibility of Social Media Manager.

KNDS was in bad shape. The team decided to rework the graphics and web presence to bring the station into the future.

I created KNDS's new logo and visual identity. Below is the main KNDS logo and the alternate design used for their staff shirts.

I was inspired by the old KNDS logo, and used the same color as a homage to KNDS's roots. The original sketch from the general manager took inspiration from vinyl records and audio waveforms. The idea of vinyl records stayed strong throughout my design process, with the main logo featuring consecutive circles to imitate the grooves of a record. For the staff shirt, the circles were replaced with a bold wavy loop, imitating both vinyl record grooves and an audio waveform.

When it came to choosing a font, I knew I wanted a font similar to Impact. However, I wanted something with more character. I was able to find a font called Mikodacs. It fit everything I was looking for. Mikodacs is bold and unique, perfect for a radio station focused on indie music. Additionally, the font is licensed under the SIL Open Font License, allowing for free commercial usage. Very important for a student-run, non-profit organization.

I carried the same font and general style throughout all my work with KNDS. I created two 3D-printed handouts. A radio figurine for people involved with the staion, and a keychain to hand out at public events.


I custom modeled these printouts for KNDS. I used a combination of Blender and PrusaSlicer for the radio figurine, and purely PrusaSlicer for the keychain. The models were printed for free, courtesy of the NDSU Digital Fabrication Lab.
